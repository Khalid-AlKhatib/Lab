ArgsBase Lab

AI for reasoning, critical thinking, and society

ArgsBase Lab studies and builds reasoning-centered language technologies that help people and AI reason more critically about complex issues. Our work connects human–AI reasoning, deliberation, computational argumentation, multilingual AI, and societal NLP.

News

Latest from the lab

  • October 2026

    Daleel 2026 at ArabicNLP / EMNLP

    We co-organised the Daleel shared task with QatarDebate. Twelve teams participated in Arabic argument mining on editorials and debates, and the overview paper is now published.

  • October 2026

    Qing Wang joins the group

    Qing Wang joined as a PhD researcher. Her project studies how readers interpret AI in science fiction and how these perspectives can inform AI evaluation.

  • March 2026

    ArgsBase demo at EACL 2026

    Our ArgsBase system demonstration paper appeared at EACL 2026. It describes a multi-agent interface for structured human–AI deliberation.

  • February 2026

    ARGUS preprint on narratives and persuasion

    Sara Nabhani and collaborators released ARGUS, a study of how narrative features relate to persuasion in online arguments.
