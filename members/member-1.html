Faculty

Khalid Al-Khatib

Assistant Professor in NLP · University of Groningen

Khalid Al-Khatib works on computational argumentation and discourse analysis. He develops NLP methods and datasets for analyzing arguments and explores how AI can support reasoning and critical thinking.

Background

  • 2022–present: Assistant Professor, University of Groningen.
  • 2020–2021: Postdoctoral researcher, Leipzig University and Bauhaus-Universität Weimar.
  • Earlier: Research positions at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, IBM Research Ireland, and the University of Stuttgart.
  • Education: PhD in Computer Science (Bauhaus-Universität Weimar) and MSc in Computer Science (Jordan University of Science and Technology).
